Globe land dots sampled on a 2-degree latitude grid from Natural Earth 110m land polygons (public domain). Longitude spacing is adjusted by latitude to keep the dots evenly spaced. Polygon holes are excluded.

Source: https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/geojson/ne_110m_land.geojson
License: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/

Coordinates are [latitude, longitude]. Testimonial node positions are illustrative, not customer locations.
